# Builder Round submission: 1AM

**Link:** <your-vercel-url>

**Who it's for:** anyone with a health worry they'd never say out loud, the kind people type into a search bar at 1 AM: hair in the drain, a late period, waking at 3 AM, a toddler who won't eat.

**What it does:** it answers that moment in about 60 seconds, against the thresholds specialists actually use (AAD, ACOG, Cleveland Clinic, NSF, AASM, AAP and others), with the source on every answer. You get a plain verdict (normal / keep an eye on it / worth a doctor visit), the red flags, one thing to try tonight, and a doctor-ready summary. An optional 14-day tracker turns one anxious data point into a trend. No sign-up, nothing to buy, nothing leaves the phone.

**What makes it interesting:**
- It's built on Mosaic's own insight that health is pull, not push: the moment of worry comes first, and trust earned there is what makes a long relationship possible.
- Brand quizzes end in a cart. 1AM often ends in "you're fine".
- A tap-to-count hair ritual, Hinglish free-text ("baal bahut gir rahe hain"), and seeded examples so you can see the retention payoff in seconds.
- Deterministic and private by design: no chatbot, no made-up statistics, and a one-tap wipe for shared phones.

- Claude Code: built the UI from my spec. The medical logic was written and tested first.
